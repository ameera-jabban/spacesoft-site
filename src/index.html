Frequently asked

What is Space Soft ERP?

A web-based ERP platform that connects accounting and core operations in one controlled system, helping businesses replace fragmented records and gain clearer management information.

Is the system cloud-based?

The current deployment direction is a web application hosted in the customer's private or on-premise environment. Managed cloud availability is confirmed per commercial offer.

Can it support multiple companies and branches?

Yes. The system is designed for multi-company and multi-branch structures, with access and reporting configured according to your approved organization setup.

Does it support multiple currencies?

Yes. Multi-currency processing is part of the product, with exchange-rate and reporting behavior configured to your finance requirements.

Can the system be customized?

Yes. Forms, fields, workflows, permissions and reports can be configured or developed based on a documented scope and technical assessment.

Does it support Arabic and English?

Yes. The product includes Arabic and English interfaces and terminology.

How long does implementation take?

Timing depends on company size, modules, data migration, customization, integrations, training and user acceptance. A schedule is provided after discovery and scope confirmation.

Are training and support provided?

Yes. Implementation guidance, administrator and user training, go-live support and post-launch support are provided under the agreed service plan.
